# Research Agent — Topics, Trends, Evidence and References

**Status:** Separate Research service is DECIDED; detailed workflows are PROPOSED. An offline library foundation (`orchestrator/research/`: provider abstraction, source scoring, claim status, briefs) is implemented and unit-tested on synthetic data; it has no live providers and is not wired into the pipeline (M-RSR-01 in `PROJECT_STATUS.json`).

## 1. Mission

Find timely and evergreen material that can inspire or strengthen videos while preserving the creator’s own point of view and authentic footage.

## 2. Research categories

- Current trends and popular formats.
- Recent events and public-interest topics.
- Scientific discoveries and credible explanations.
- Mycology, biology, DIY and technology information.
- Interesting questions, myths and misconceptions.
- Successful English-language videos/formats that may be adapted for Croatian audiences.
- Professional editing examples and educational sources on storytelling, retention, attention, comprehension and humor.

## 3. Research brief format

Each research brief should contain:
- Topic and potential audience.
- Why it may be interesting now.
- Main factual claims.
- Primary/authoritative sources where possible.
- Publication dates and retrieval dates.
- Disagreements, uncertainty and limitations.
- Potential angles and visual explanations.
- Possible hook/payoff concepts.
- How the idea relates to available footage or a possible future shoot.
- What is fact, what is interpretation and what is opinion.

## 4. Source quality

Prefer primary research, official documentation, original reporting, recognized scientific/educational institutions and direct source material. Use social/community discussion to understand audience reactions or discover leads, not as sole proof of factual claims.

Check dates for current-events research. Do not present old information as current.

## 5. Adaptation, not copying

A successful foreign video can be studied for:
- Topic framing.
- Story structure.
- Demonstration method.
- Visual explanation.
- Pacing and audience promise.

Do not copy a creator’s exact script, footage, graphics, branding or distinctive expression. Adapt the underlying idea or format into the user’s own context and voice.

## 6. Editorial independence

Research should not force the creator into a trend or manufacture an opinion. Present multiple relevant perspectives when the topic is contested. Clearly label uncertain or disputed claims.

## 7. Outputs

- Topic opportunity brief.
- Fact-check packet.
- Reference-video analysis.
- Editing research note.
- Visual research brief for Asset Agent.
- Source list with citations and dates.
